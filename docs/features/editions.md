# Editions

**Features:** Edition lifecycle, Home, Archive
**Core journey:** yes (Organizer runs an edition into the archive; everyone lands on home)

## Rules

### Lifecycle
- An edition is either teams or free-for-all.
- An edition is **Setup**, **Live** or **Ended**. An Organizer moves it from one to the next.
  - **Setup:** visible to Organizers only, so Jason can build teams, competitions and pages before anyone sees them.
  - **Live:** visible to everyone. Usually from when teams are announced, weeks before the week itself, so hosts can set up and run qualifiers.
  - **Ended:** has a winner and is in the archive. Organizers can still edit it (e.g. backfilling War Week XI, entering awards); hosts and participants are read-only.
- One edition is Live at a time. A Setup edition can exist alongside Live and Ended ones.
- **Ending** an edition finishes any open Participation and Best score competitions; their current points become final. Unfinished Ranking, Bracket, Heats and Survival competitions stay unfinished and award no points until an Organizer records the rest. Before confirming, the Organizer sees what's still open and what ending will do.
- An Organizer can move an Ended edition back to Live (e.g. ended by mistake mid-week), if no other edition is Live. Competitions finished by ending stay finished. Hosts and participants get back what they have in any Live edition.

### Home
- Home shows the current edition: the Live one, else the most recently Ended. A Setup edition never appears on home.
- Standings, recent results across competitions, and links to the edition's pages. An Ended edition shows its winner.
- A section at the top lists the competitions you host and your open matches where you can self-report. It appears only when it has something in it.

### Archive
- Lists every Ended edition. Opening one shows the same screens as the current edition (home, competitions, pages).
