# War Week

Rebuild of War Week, the app for Jahnel Group's yearly War Week: live standings, results and history.

## Docs
- `docs/requirements.md`: profile, roles, features and their status, Deferred items.
- `docs/features/`: `competitions.md` (formats, scoring, self-report, standings), `editions.md` (lifecycle, home, install, archive, awards), `people.md` (sign-in incl. partners, roster, person page, Organizers).
- `docs/architecture.md`: stack, structure and decisions.
- The [screens canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/JNYqMR3uwFtcJrrRYv98eo) holds every approved screen; each feature doc says which parts are its own.

## Boundaries
- The previous version lives at `~/Code/jg-war-week-proto`. Use it to see how things worked, not as a source of requirements or code. Nothing carries over unless it's in the docs above.
